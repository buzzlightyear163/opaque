/threat

        
Solana has no mempool. It still has sandwiches.

        
There is no public waiting room on Solana, but an order still passes through RPC nodes, relays and the validator building the block before it lands. Anyone on that path who can read it can trade ahead of it.

/protocol

        
The key doesn't exist yet.

        
OPAQUE uses drand's timelock encryption. Your order is locked to a round number, and the only key that opens it is drand's signature on that round. Nobody can produce it early.

      


      

        
    
          
  1. 
            
    01t₀
    
            
    Lock
    
            
    Your browser encrypts the order to round N. The round number is the identity and drand's public key is the master key. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    
          
    2. 
          
  2. 
            
    02t₀ → tₙ
    
            
    Wait
    
            
    The key is drand's BLS signature on round N. A threshold of independent operators produces it, and it does not exist before that round. quicknet launched with 18 organizations running 22 nodes.
    
          
    3. 
          
  3. 
            
    03tₙ
    
            
    Open
    
            
    drand publishes a signature every 3 seconds. When round N's lands, anyone can open the order, and everyone gets it at the same instant.

/batch

        
The line is set before anyone can read it.

        
Orders sealed to the same round take their place in line while they are still ciphertext. They open together and run in that order, so once the contents are visible there is no slot left to jump into.
